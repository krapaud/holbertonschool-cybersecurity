# Section 4.2: Prohibited Activities

> Users shall not use BioHealth's systems, networks, devices, accounts,
> computing resources, or data for cryptocurrency mining or any related
> activity. This prohibition applies regardless of whether the activity is
> performed during working hours, outside working hours, or from a remote
> location.
>
> Users shall not install, download, copy, modify, or run software on BioHealth
> systems without prior authorization from the Information Technology or
> Security team. This includes software, scripts, packages, browser
> extensions, and tools obtained from unauthorized sources.
>
> Users shall not attempt to bypass security controls, use another person's
> account, or use company resources for activities that could reduce system
> performance, create security risks, violate applicable law, or interfere
> with BioHealth's business operations.
>
> BioHealth may monitor and investigate suspected violations of this policy.
> A violation may result in the immediate suspension or removal of access
> privileges, disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment
> or contract, recovery of associated costs, and referral to law enforcement or
> other legal action where appropriate. BioHealth may also pursue civil
> remedies for losses or damage caused by the violation.
